Hi! I'm Giorgos, an AI/ML Engineer at Accenture with a background in systems and machine learning. I earned my Diploma in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the [National Technical University of Athens](https://ntua.gr/).

My [thesis](http://artemis.cslab.ece.ntua.gr:8080/jspui/handle/123456789/20036) focused on shell benchmarking and was supervised by Prof. [Nikos Vasilakis](https://nikos.vasilak.is/) (Brown University) and Prof. [Georgios Goumas](http://www.cslab.ntua.gr/~goumas/) (NTUA).

Before joining Accenture, I worked as a Machine Learning Research Intern at the [Archimedes Research Unit](https://archimedesai.gr/en/), under the supervision of Prof. [Evaggelia Pitoura](https://archimedesai.gr/en/researchers/evaggelia-pitoura).

I am interested in operating systems, programming languages, and leveraging ML to build high-performance systems.

Outside of work and research, I enjoy hiking, discovering new music, and reading.
